How I work

Software comes after understanding the operation.

I do not start with a framework and search for a use case. I start with the business process: what is slow, repetitive, error-prone, invisible to management, or difficult for the team to execute consistently.

01

Map the workflow

Understand people, handoffs, data, exceptions, and existing tools.

02

Define the rules

Convert business logic into requirements, validations, states, and measurable outcomes.

03

Design the system

Choose the simplest architecture that can support the workflow reliably.

04

Build and improve

Implement, test against real usage, fix friction, and automate where the economics make sense.
